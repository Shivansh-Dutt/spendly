Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you register, such as your name and email address, and the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type, to keep the service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate Spendly, show you your spending summaries, secure your account and improve the service. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers we control and is retained for as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored in hashed form. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data and expense records, except where we are required by law to keep them.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to display text. Loading these fonts may share your IP address with Google. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, please contact us at the support email listed on the Spendly website.
